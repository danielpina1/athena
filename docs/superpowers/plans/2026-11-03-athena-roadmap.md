# Athena roadmap

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-athena-design.md`

The spec covers several subsystems, so it is built as a sequence of plans. Each plan ends in working, tested software and is written just before it starts, so it can use what the previous plans actually produced.

Basic structured logging and `athena status` / `athena logs` arrive in plan 1; plan 9 builds the full visibility layer on the events every earlier plan already emits.

Plans 10 and 11 were added on 2026-11-04 (ideas from nucleos, spec §18). Plan numbers are names, not the build order: plan 10 is built **after plan 4** (so its dashboard controls exist) and before plan 5, because the brakes and trust classes matter as soon as Athena works on its own repository; plan 11 follows plan 10. Plan 9's hard spend brake needs the usage data plan 9 collects.

**Plan 10 is built** (branch `p10-autonomy-brakes`; reference: [autonomy.md](../../dev/autonomy.md)): the waiting-on-you brake per repository, the kill switch (`athena pause` / `athena resume` and the dashboard control, resume behind re-authentication), trust classes with the `restricted` tool policy, shadow mode per repository (held for you, with Proposals applied from the node), and Approval-promotion offers. Two parts of the plan 10 row wait on later plans: the **hard spend brake** needs the usage data plan 9 collects (plan 10 brakes on what waits on the owner, not on spend), and the **local-model summary** of untrusted text needs plan 8's inference backend (until then untrusted text reaches the worker marked as data, not summarised).

**Plan 11 is built** (branch `p11-autopilot`; reference: [autonomy.md](../../dev/autonomy.md) section 9): the `schedule` Source with cron entries in local time and repository events (`branch_moved`, `manifest_changed`, `ci_red`), each entry its own rule (`autopilot/<slug>`) with owner trust, edited in Settings > Autopilot and braked by the waiting-on-you brake, the kill switch and shadow mode. An entry runs one at a time, fires at least 15 minutes apart and at most 24 times a day, and after downtime runs only its latest missed firing.

**Plan 6 is built** (branch `p6-multi-node`; reference: [nodes.md](../../dev/nodes.md); deferrals: [p6-deferred](2026-11-10-athena-p6-deferred.md)): `athena node join` and `athena node run` put a second machine behind the server over mutual TLS, the fleet places each Assignment (pin, warmth, load) and shows every node on the dashboard, an offline node's Tickets say so, and Retry elsewhere moves repository work to another node from its last checkpoint. Node idle RSS and remote dispatch have budgets in `make bench`. The second-machine smoke test is the owner's step. Quota per account across nodes, labels, node-to-node git and Dir Ticket moves are deferred (see the deferred file).

**Plan 7 is built** (branch `p7-agents-split`; reference: [agents.md](../../dev/agents.md); deferrals: [p7-deferred](2026-11-11-athena-p7-deferred.md)): a lead Ticket can have child Tickets with deps, made by the worker tool `hand_off` or by an approved plan. Plan first has the planner (`internal/planner`, strategies `llm` and `off`) draft a breakdown that the owner edits and approves in the dashboard's split editor. Workers get `ask_agent`, `wait_for` and `agents`, with hop and rate limits set in Policy `split`. Child branches cross nodes through the server's mirror, and the node link is Protocol 2. The splitting smoke test is the owner's step. The graph view of a draft, the Policy and router triggers for plan first, capability targets, node-to-node git, pruning of remote child branches and automatic merging are deferred (see the deferred file).
